Voice listening test

About 6 minutes. Nothing leaves this computer. / 约 6 分钟，数据不会离开这台电脑。

You will hear N short clips of a tutor explaining a computer-science idea, about 16 seconds each. For every clip, give two ratings from 1 to 5:

  • Naturalness: does it sound like a real person talking?
  • Continuity: does it sound like one continuous speech, or like separate sentences put one after another?

Use headphones. Set a comfortable volume once and leave it. You can replay a clip twice. There are no right answers, and some clips are much worse than others, so use the whole scale.

你会听到 N 段助教讲解计算机概念的短录音，每段约 16 秒。每段请打两个 1 到 5 的分：自然度（像不像真人在说话）和连贯度（是一整段连续的话，还是一句一句拼起来的）。戴耳机，音量调一次就不要再动。每段可以再听两遍。没有标准答案，有些录音会明显更差，请用满整个量表。
